Spellbinding AR & Retail App

To deliver an immersive omnichannel shopping experience, SPELLBINDING TOYS AND GAMES LTD develops and maintains an official mobile application.

The Spellbinding App bridges our physical stores with digital interactivity. Customers can browse our entire inventory of toys and games, use the built-in AR scanner to see 3D toy models in-store, and manage their retail loyalty rewards directly from their Android devices.

  • AR Scanner: Point your camera at toy boxes to see them come alive.
  • E-Commerce: Order sporting goods and hobbies for home delivery.
  • Digital Loyalty Card: Earn "Spells" (points) on every retail purchase.
  • In-Store Stock Checker: Instantly verify if a game is on the shelf.
